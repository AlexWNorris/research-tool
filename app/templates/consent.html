Web privacy for whom: Demographic Vulnerability to browser fingerprinting

You are being invited to take part in an academic research study.
Before you decide to participate in this study, it is important that you understand why the research is being done and what it will involve. Please take the time to read the following information carefully.

Purpose of the research

The purpose of the research is to understand if different people are more at risk of being tracked on the internet than others.

Who is doing this research?

The lead researcher of this study is Alexander Norris, Alexander is an undergraduate student in the department of computer science. There are no other researchers involved in this study.

Why have I been approached?

This research aims to have a sample of people that represent users on the internet, this includes people of different ages, genders and backgrounds.

This research aims to recruit one thousand research participants.

Procedures of this study

Your role in this study should take no longer than 10 minutes. You will be asked 4 questions about your: Age, Gender, Income and Education. You will then be asked permission to collect some information about your web browser. You will be shown the full list of this information and will be able to decide whether you would like to share it for use in this research.

Benefits and risks of taking part

There is unlikely to be any direct or personal benefit to you in taking part. There may be some benefit to society; but this cannot be assured at the start of the research project. There is some risk in taking part in this survey. If a data breach occurs, your information may be used by third parties to identify you on the internet.

Voluntary participation and your right to withdraw

Your participation in this study is entirely voluntary. You are under no obligation to take part, and you may stop at any time. If you stop before completing the survey your information will not be saved and you will not have completed the survey. Withdrawal after participation is possible and can be done via email request to an631@exeter.ac.uk.

Anonymity

Your name is not required in this survey. There will be question about some personal characteristics such as age, gender income and education. The information collected about your web browser, may have the capability to identify you on the internet.

The Data Controller for this research is the University of Exeter. You may contact the Information Governance office of the University of Exeter by emailing dataprotection@exeter.ac.uk.
